◆ Simple by design

Three steps. One great GIF.

01

Add your frames

Drag in as many PNGs or JPGs as you like, or browse for them. Every image becomes a frame — sorted by filename and kept on your device.

02

Order & tune

Drag thumbnails into the perfect sequence. Set the frame delay, colors, size, loop count, and background.

03

Export instantly

Hit Create GIF. Encoding happens locally in a Web Worker, then your GIF is ready to download and share.
